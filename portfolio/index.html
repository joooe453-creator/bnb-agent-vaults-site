Vault value is cash plus the current value of held tokens and lending positions. Share value estimates the value represented by one share, roughly vault value divided by all shares. Contract adjustments and rounding apply; this estimate excludes exit costs and can change before a transaction.

When you leave

  • USDTRedeem a proportional slice with a minimum received. If the route or minimum cannot be met, the transaction reverts.
  • In kindReceive your slice of tokens and lending receipts without a USDT conversion. You manage their subsequent redemption and market risk.
  • SeedOnly creator seed shares have a 90-day lock; your unlocked shares remain readable when vault value is unavailable.

Before a wider mandate

24-hour delay

A higher investment limit has to be announced on chain and wait 24 hours. Review the pending change and the actual exit cost before deciding whether to stay.
