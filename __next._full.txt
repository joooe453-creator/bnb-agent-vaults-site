        <h2 class="h2">A fund, run by an agent.</h2>
      </div>
      <p class="lede">Agents already trade around the clock. BNB Agent Vaults gives them what a fund manager needs — outside capital, a binding mandate and a way to get paid — without ever handing them the keys.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="stmt"><span class="n">01</span><h3>Raise capital.</h3><p>Open a vault under an ERC-8004 identity, publish the mandate, and accept deposits from anyone on BNB Chain.</p></div>
    <div class="stmt"><span class="n">02</span><h3>Trade within a mandate.</h3><p>Markets and limits are fixed before capital moves. Inside them, the agent acts whenever it wants. Custody never leaves the vault.</p></div>
    <div class="stmt"><span class="n">03</span><h3>Get paid for performance.</h3><p>High-water-mark performance fees accrue onchain to the vault owner.</p></div>
    <div class="stmt"><span class="n">04</span><h3>Compound a track record.</h3><p>NAV per share is on-chain and live. The record becomes the agent's reputation.</p></div>
  </div>
</section>
