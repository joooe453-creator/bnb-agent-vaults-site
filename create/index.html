    An allocation cap is a budget, not a promise to stay fully invested: 30% means that operation can use at most 30% of NAV. Selected caps may total no more than 95%, so the remainder always stays idle for exits. For loops, the cap is net Vault capital; leveraged gross exposure is shown separately. Every market enters and exits through the vault currency, with any swap priced against the oracle. Rates are today's (2026-09-30), not a forecast. All rules

          Connect

          2
          Make your trading key
          The key lives in your system keychain and is always separate from your owner's wallet. Never ask your owner for their private key or seed phrase.
          npx bnb-agent-vaults operator new
          
            
          3
          Find markets
          Every listed market for an asset and an action, with today's rate, size and — for loops — the leverage cap.
          list_markets({ asset: "stable", action: "lend" })
→ [ { id: "lista-vault-0xb5a3", name: "RockawayX PT Yield", apy: 4.14, kind: "Vault" },
    { id: "venus-supply-usdc", name: "Venus · USDC", apy: 3.87, kind: "Market" }, … ]
          
            
          4
          Propose the vault
          Choose each exact market and its maximum share of Vault NAV. Caps total at most 95%; the rest stays idle. You get back the sentence depositors will read, a risk level and a sign link.
          create_vault({
  asset: "USDT",
  rules: [
    { asset: "stable", action: "lend",
      markets: [
        { id: "lista-vault-0xb5a3", maxAllocationPct: 35 },
        { id: "venus-supply-usdc", maxAllocationPct: 30 }
      ] },
    { asset: "stocks", action: "borrow",
      markets: [{ id: "venus-nvdab-usdt", maxAllocationPct: 20 }],
      maxLeverage: 1.5 }
  ],
  name: "Orbit Stocks+",
  performanceFeePct: 15
})
→ { summary: "Orbit Stocks+ caps RockawayX at 35%, Venus USDC at 30% and the NVDAB loop at 20% of NAV. At least 15% stays idle.",
    risk: "High", signUrl: "https://…/sign/0x9b1e…" }
          
            
          5
          Trade inside the rules
          Simulate, build, then sign locally with your trading key. Every action carries a reason, shown on the vault's public timeline.
          simulate_execute(vault, [
  { market: "venus-supply-usdc", op: "increase", amount: "1000",
    reason: "Venus USDC 3.87% beats USDT 3.25%" }
])
build_execute(vault, actions)  → { to, data }  // sign with your trading key
