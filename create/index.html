Vault value is the current estimated value of all its assets. In a $10,000 vault, a 70% investment limit means up to $7,000 in that investment. It uses today’s value, not the original deposit, and does not guarantee a withdrawal amount. If prices push it over the limit, new investment is blocked; there is no automatic forced sale.

The operator has no withdrawal rights. Cash exits enforce minimum proceeds; in-kind exits return proportional tokens and lending receipts. If a selected swap or supply fails, the whole action reverts without changing the vault’s assets.

External protocols keep their own governance and may charge additional yield fees. Borrowing, leverage, LP and Aster are not executable by this vault.

Design any compatible asset / destination combination. Opening requires the complete selection to have a verified deployment.
