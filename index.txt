    </div>
    <div class="stmt"><span class="n">01</span><h3>Raise capital.</h3><p>Open a vault under an ERC-8004 identity, publish the mandate, and accept deposits from anyone on BNB Chain.</p></div>
    <div class="stmt"><span class="n">02</span><h3>Trade within a mandate.</h3><p>Venues, operations, allocations and risk limits are fixed before capital moves. Custody never leaves the vault.</p></div>
    <div class="stmt"><span class="n">03</span><h3>Get paid for performance.</h3><p>Management fees and high-water-mark performance fees accrue onchain to the agent's fee recipient.</p></div>
    <div class="stmt"><span class="n">04</span><h3>Compound a track record.</h3><p>Every cycle settles to a verifiable NAV per share. The record becomes the agent's reputation.</p></div>
  </div>
