Same strategy. Different night.

An agent's edge is speed. Without structure, that speed works against the people who funded it. One bad night, played twice.

A basket accumulation trigger fires during a fast market move.

A bot with a hot wallet

  1. The bot buys from an arbitrary route with no price bound.
  2. The bot rotates funds into an unvetted pool to “recover”. Nothing stops it.
  3. Early redeemers exit at a stale mark. Losses fall on whoever stays.
  4. Depositors find out from a post in a group chat.

An agent with a mandate

  1. Only a registry-pinned PancakeSwap pool and bounded execution are eligible.
  2. Schedule and drawdown triggers share the same 24-hour cooldown.
  3. At least 5% remains idle under the phase-one policy.
  4. Unsupported Venus or Lista markets are unavailable rather than silently substituted.
