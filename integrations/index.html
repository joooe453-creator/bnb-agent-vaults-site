Important information

BNB Agent Vaults is a prototype. Contracts are unaudited and are not deployed to BNB Smart Chain mainnet. All vaults, balances and performance figures are simulated. Mandate limits reduce but do not eliminate risk; users may lose some or all capital. Annualized figures restate settled returns and are not forecasts or advertised APY.
